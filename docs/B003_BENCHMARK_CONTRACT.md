# Benchmark 003 — Straight Through Slot Contract

The part is a 100 × 60 × 20 mm block with one centred X-major straight through slot.
`overall_length_mm = 40` is the end-to-end extent. The width and end radius are 20 mm and 10 mm; therefore centre-to-centre length is 20 mm. Angles are degrees, zero is +X, and direction is explicitly `CW` or `CCW`.

The face-on slot is represented as two tangent lines plus two semicircular arcs. Orthogonal hidden supports must prove the 40 mm longitudinal extent, 20 mm width, full 20 mm depth span, and the structured through-state evidence.
