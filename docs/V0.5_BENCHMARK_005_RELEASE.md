## Scope

B005 acceptance applies only to **BaseBlock + Offset Through Hole + Offset Straight Slot**:

- Base: 100 × 60 × 20 mm.
- Hole: Ø20, model centre (-25,-10), Z-axis through hole.
- Slot: L40 × W20, R10, centre (15,8), X-major through slot.
- Hole and Slot are disjoint and independently depend on Base.

Validated for defined benchmark scope. Complex parts, intersecting cuts, arbitrary
feature counts, and arbitrary three-view drawings remain outside this acceptance.
The historical Phase 3 runner retains its `PASS_CANDIDATE` classification; this
release review records that its required gates were satisfied on the merged baseline.

## Ownership chain

Part: `IFace2.GetFeature → GetPersistReference3 → feature_id`.
Drawing: resolve model feature persistent reference, map with `IView.GetCorresponding`,
then trace `GetPolylines7 → IEdge.GetTwoAdjacentFaces2 / ISilhouetteEdge.GetFace →
IFace2.GetFeature`, comparing view-context COM identity.
Only `API_EXACT` passes. Names and nearest geometry are not ownership evidence.
Visibility comes from `HLR_CAPTURE` and `HLV_MINUS_HLR`.
